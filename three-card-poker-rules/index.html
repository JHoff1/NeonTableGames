Three Card Poker

Rules and Strategy

Three Card Poker gives you one decision against the Dealer: match your Ante with a Play wager or fold. Pair Plus is a separate optional wager based only on your three-card hand.

Play Three Card Poker

Rules

How a Round Works

1. Place the wagers

An Ante is required. Pair Plus is optional and can be any available chip amount.

2. Deal three cards

You see your three cards while the Dealer hand remains face down.

3. Play or fold

Play adds a wager equal to your Ante. Fold forfeits the Ante; Pair Plus still resolves independently.

4. Compare hands

The Dealer reveals. A Dealer hand needs queen-high or better to qualify.

Three-Card Hand Rankings

With only three cards, a straight is less common than a flush, so it ranks higher. A-2-3 is the lowest straight; Q-K-A is the highest.

Dealer Qualification and Ante/Play

Dealer does not qualify

Your Ante pays 1 to 1 and the Play wager pushes.

Dealer qualifies and you win

Ante and Play each pay 1 to 1.

Hands tie

Ante and Play both push.

Dealer wins

Ante and Play both lose. Ante Bonus can still pay when you chose Play.

Strategy

The Q-6-4 Decision

Play Q-6-4 or better

  1. Always Play a pair or better.
  2. Play every king-high or ace-high hand.
  3. With queen-high, Play Q-7-x or better.
  4. With exactly Q-6, Play when the third card is 4 or higher; otherwise Fold.
  5. Fold all jack-high or lower hands that do not contain a pair.

The table shows this recommendation after each deal. Pair Plus does not change the optimal Ante/Play decision because it resolves separately.

Glossary

Three Card Poker Terms

Ante

The required opening wager against the Dealer.

Play

A second wager equal to the Ante, placed when you continue the hand.

Pair Plus

An optional wager that pays on a pair or better without comparing against the Dealer.

Qualify

The Dealer has queen-high or better, allowing Ante and Play to be compared normally.

Ante Bonus

An additional payment for a straight, three of a kind, or straight flush after you Play.

Push

A tied or non-competing wager returned without a win or loss.
