Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

Neon Table Games provides free browser-based play-money games. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the site.

Information We Collect

Neon Table Games does not ask you to create an account and does not collect payment information. The games may store basic local game state in your browser, such as your current play-money bankroll or best run, so the experience can continue between visits.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, including pages visited, general traffic sources, device information, and interactions such as visiting the blackjack game or tutorial. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers to provide these measurements.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use Google services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertising

If ads are added to Neon Table Games, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads. Any ads should remain separate from gameplay controls and should not require you to click them to use the site.

Free Play-Money Games

Neon Table Games does not offer real-money gambling. There are no deposits, wagers of real value, prizes, or cash-out options. Any dollar amounts shown in games are fictional play-money values for entertainment and practice only.

Your Choices

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also clear local site data in your browser to remove saved play-money game progress.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method provided by Neon Table Games when one is made available.
